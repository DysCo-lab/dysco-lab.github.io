Research Statement

Our research spans dynamics, control, data-driven modeling, and human-centered systems, integrating theory, computation, and experiments across aerospace, mechanical, and biomedical domains. Experimental validation follows strong theoretical modeling.

Thermoacoustics

System Identification

Identification of thermoacoustic instability mechanisms using reduced-order and data-driven models.

Data-driven Modelling

Machine learning and operator-theoretic approaches for thermoacoustic dynamics.

Learn to Fly

5-DOF Gimbal Structure

Experimental platforms for flight dynamics and control validation.

Data Acquisition

High-rate sensing and synchronization for aerial experiments.

Wind Wall

Unsteady Aerodynamics

Flow-field characterization and force estimation in confined wind environments.

System Control

Feedback control of flow–structure interaction systems.

Large-scale PIV

High-resolution velocity field measurements.

VYOM

Biomechanical Intelligence

Integrated EMG–IMU–ECG sensing with adaptive learning for human performance and rehabilitation.

Physiological Signals

Complex-systems analysis of cardiovascular and neuromuscular dynamics.

Army Research

Volutpat

Defense-oriented energy, mobility, and sensing systems.

Elementum Tempus

Time-dependent system analysis for field-deployable platforms.
