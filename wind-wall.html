Wind Wall

Unsteady Aerodynamics and Flow Visualization Platform

Overview

Wind Wall is a research project in unsteady aerodynamics focused on the development of a controlled flow-generation and visualization platform for large-scale experiments. The module combines actuation, control, structural integration, and diagnostic methods to support experimental studies in flow physics.

The project is being carried out in collaboration with DRDO-DYSL-AT, under the guidance of the coordinating scientist Mr. V. V. S. M. Ganesh. The work includes remotely actuated bubble generation for flow visualization, fan system design, flight stability analysis, large-scale PIV, and embedded control implementation.

Disciplines

  • Unsteady Aerodynamics
  • System Control
  • PWM-based Control using FPGA and STM32
  • Large-Scale PIV
  • Flight Stability Analysis
  • Fan Design and Dynamics
  • Structural Design

Methodologies

  • FPGA-based PWM control
  • STM32 embedded control
  • Large-scale PIV diagnostics
  • Structural design and integration
  • Fan system design and dynamics analysis
  • Flow visualization using remotely actuated bubble generation
