Observe. Think. Wonder.

At the Dynamics and Control Lab, we explore theoretical formalisms and substantiate them with experiments. Our research spans complex systems, fluid dynamics, nonlinear dynamics, and thermoacoustics.

Research Focus

Three pillars that organize our research programs and student projects.

Thermoacoustics and Instabilities

System identification, reduced order models, and data-driven prediction for thermoacoustic systems and flutter.

System Identification Model Order Reduction Stability

Dynamical Systems and Control

Control and learning strategies for dynamical systems, with an inclination to aerial robotics.

Aerial robotics Optimal control Applied Math

Complex Systems

Fluid flows, Networks of oscillators, and human-centered system dynamics.

Networks Signals Data-driven approaches
