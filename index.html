Observe. Think. Wonder.

At the Dynamics and Control Lab, we explore fascinating theoretical formalisms and substantiate them with wonderful experiments.
Our research spans complex systems, fluid dynamics, nonlinear dynamics, and thermoacoustics, with a strong emphasis on data-driven modelling, distributed control, and the analysis of continuous dynamical systems.
