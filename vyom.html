VYOM

Analysis of physiological signals from a complex systems perspective

Overview

Vyom is a biomedical startup developing an integrated framework called Biomechanical Intelligence to understand, monitor, and optimize human movement and physiology. At its core, Vyom combines muscle activity (EMG), limb kinematics (IMU), and other physiological signals to build a coherent, real-time picture of the human body in motion. The framework intends to leverage reinforcement learning to adapt exercise and rehabilitation protocols dynamically, personalizing intensity, form, and progression to reduce injury risk and improve outcomes. In parallel, we explore characterization of ECG signals using methods from complex systems analysis, including critical transition indicators, to detect subtle dynamical changes that precede pathological events. By unifying biomechanics, physiological dynamics, and adaptive intelligence, Vyom aims to move beyond static metrics toward a deeper, systems-level understanding of human performance and health.
